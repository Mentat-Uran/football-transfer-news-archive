# Report Rules

## Daily coverage

Cover the previous calendar day from 00:00 to 23:59 in China/Taipei time unless the report explicitly states another equivalent UTC+8 label.

## Priority

1. Manchester United
2. Major Premier League clubs
3. Real Madrid, Barcelona, Bayern Munich and other major European clubs

## Source hierarchy

Highest priority:

- Club official announcements
- League and association announcements

Then:

- BBC Sport
- The Athletic
- Sky Sports
- The Guardian
- David Ornstein
- Fabrizio Romano
- Reliable club correspondents and strong local media

A reporter's reliability should be judged club-by-club. Important rumors should be cross-checked. A single reporter or low-quality source must not be written as confirmed fact.

`Here we go` or a reported club agreement may be treated as a high-confidence development, but not as a completed signing until the club announcement or registration is complete.

## Transfer stages

Use these stages consistently:

1. 官方官宣 / 完成注册
2. 接近完成
3. 达成协议 / 安排体检
4. 谈判中
5. 表达兴趣 / 初步接触
6. 低可信流言

Separate confirmed facts, media reports and analysis.

Player names should use the common Chinese translation plus the English name where practical.

## Report structure during transfer windows

1. Manchester United 重点动态
2. Manchester United 相关传闻（按可信度）
3. 英超主要转会
4. Real Madrid / Barcelona / Bayern Munich 等豪门动态
5. 接下来最值得关注的 1–3 条进展

## Weekly summary

On the scheduled weekly run, append a deduplicated seven-day summary covering:

- Manchester United completed moves, negotiations and target changes
- Key moves involving major Premier League clubs and European giants
- 3–5 major trends
- 1–3 key storylines for the following week

## Transfer-window review

On important deadline days or the first run within 24 hours after a window closes, append a whole-window review including:

- Manchester United arrivals and departures
- Net spend and squad impact
- Key moves by major Premier League clubs and Real Madrid / Barcelona / Bayern Munich
- Biggest deal, best operation, biggest risk and unfinished business
- Expected impact on the coming season

## Outside transfer windows

When major European windows are closed and more than 14 days away from opening/closing, switch from low-quality rumor collection to substantive European football news: club decisions, coaches and executives, injuries and returns, renewals, financial/compliance matters, disciplinary actions, tactical/squad changes and other material developments.

## Noise filtering

Do not fill reports with duplicate stories, clickbait, old rumors without new information or pure speculation. If a day has no substantive developments, say so explicitly.
